# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1. The full text is available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences,
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults or derogatory comments,
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission,
- any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/hdg-zero/claude-plugin-openrouter/security/advisories/new) or by contacting hdg-zero via the GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, following the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant.
